ODD Factory

SEO and website optimisation campaign for a bespoke clothing manufacturer, focused on improving organic visibility, website performance, user experience and conversions.

Project Overview

Client: ODD Factory

Industry: Bespoke Clothing Manufacturers

Website: theoddfactory.com

Organic Growth Technical SEO On-Page SEO Website Optimisation Link Building Conversion Performance

The Challenge

Low Search Visibility

ODD Factory had limited online visibility and was struggling to appear prominently in search results, making it difficult for potential customers to discover the brand organically.

Website & User Experience Issues

The website had slow loading times, difficult navigation and limited product descriptions. These issues affected both user experience and the site's ability to perform strongly in search.

Limited Social Engagement

The existing social media presence was also generating limited interaction, creating an additional opportunity to improve brand visibility and traffic.

SEO & Digital Strategy

Technical SEO & Website Performance

Improved the website architecture, loading speed and mobile experience to create a stronger technical foundation for organic search performance.

Product & On-Page Optimisation

Enhanced product descriptions with relevant target keywords to improve search visibility and provide customers with clearer information about the products.

Website Redesign

Improved the website experience through stronger product presentation, higher-quality imagery and simpler navigation, helping the site better reflect the brand and support users through their journey.

Link Building

Implemented a backlink-building strategy focused on obtaining relevant and high-quality links to strengthen website authority and support search rankings.

Social Media Strategy

Supported the campaign with regular social content including fashion advice, new products, customer testimonials and behind-the-scenes content, while actively engaging with followers.

Results

Within five months, key target keywords began appearing on Google's first page and the campaign delivered substantial growth across organic traffic and website performance.

+200% Organic Traffic Growth
+60% Conversion Rate
-30% Bounce Rate
+70% Average Session Length

Traffic Growth Evidence

Google Analytics Performance

The December-to-January analytics comparison showed particularly strong traffic growth. Organic search traffic increased by 210%, direct traffic increased by 170% and traffic from social channels increased by 356%.

Google Analytics traffic overview – December 2021 compared with January 2022.

Organic Search Performance

Google Search Console

Search Console data also showed stronger search visibility in January compared with December. Total clicks increased by 227 and impressions increased by approximately 6.97K, with an average CTR of 2.6% in January.

Conversion Performance

Conversion performance was also monitored alongside traffic and ranking improvements. Google Analytics recorded 58 conversions in January and 45 conversions in April.

Organic search contributed 21 conversions in January and 24 conversions in April, showing that search traffic continued to contribute meaningful customer actions.

January conversion performance – 58 total conversions.

April conversion performance – 45 total conversions.

Key Outcome

The campaign improved ODD Factory's search visibility, organic traffic and website experience while also supporting measurable conversion performance.

The project demonstrates how technical SEO, product optimisation, website improvements, link building and wider digital activity can work together to support sustainable organic growth.
