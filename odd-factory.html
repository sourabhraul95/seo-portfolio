ODD Factory

SEO campaign focused on technical improvements, on-page optimisation, content strategy and organic growth, with a strong emphasis on increasing qualified website traffic and improving conversions.

Project Overview

Client: ODD Factory

Project Focus: Organic Search Growth & Conversion Improvement

Organic Growth Technical SEO On-Page SEO Content Strategy Keyword Optimisation Conversion Growth

The Challenge

The website needed stronger organic search visibility and more qualified traffic from relevant non-branded searches.

Key pages required improvements across technical SEO, metadata, content structure, keyword targeting and internal linking.

The project also focused on improving the quality of organic traffic so that stronger search visibility could contribute to better website conversions.

SEO Strategy

Technical SEO

Reviewed the website for technical issues affecting crawlability, indexation, page performance and overall search visibility.

Technical improvements were used to create a stronger foundation for organic growth.

On-Page Optimisation

Optimised important pages through improved metadata, heading structures, keyword targeting and internal linking.

Page content was aligned more closely with relevant search intent to improve ranking potential.

Keyword Strategy

Identified relevant search opportunities and prioritised keywords with stronger commercial and organic growth potential.

The strategy focused on improving visibility across both existing rankings and new keyword opportunities.

Content Strategy

Improved existing content and identified opportunities to strengthen topical relevance across important pages.

Content optimisation was supported by clearer page structure and stronger internal linking.

Conversion Focus

SEO performance was reviewed alongside website conversion data to understand whether increased organic visibility was also supporting meaningful business outcomes.

Results

The campaign delivered substantial growth in organic performance while also improving conversion outcomes.

+200% Organic Traffic Growth

+60% Conversion Improvement
