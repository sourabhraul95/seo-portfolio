Nirvana Lightings

SEO campaign focused on improving organic visibility, website traffic and conversion performance through technical SEO, on-page optimisation and content improvements.

Project Overview

Client: Nirvana Lightings

Industry: Lighting

Project Focus: Organic Search & Conversion Performance

Organic SEO Technical SEO On-Page SEO Content Strategy Keyword Optimisation Conversion Analysis

The Challenge

Nirvana Lightings needed stronger organic visibility and more relevant traffic from search engines.

The website required improvements across technical SEO, page optimisation, keyword targeting and content structure to make important pages easier for search engines and users to understand.

Alongside traffic growth, conversion performance was monitored to understand how users were interacting with the website after arriving through digital channels.

SEO Strategy

Technical SEO

Reviewed technical elements affecting website crawlability, indexation, metadata, page structure and overall organic search performance.

On-Page SEO

Optimised important pages through improved page titles, meta descriptions, headings, content structure and internal linking.

Keyword Research & Targeting

Researched relevant search terms and aligned important pages with keywords that matched the products, services and search intent of potential customers.

Content Optimisation

Improved existing website content to strengthen topical relevance and provide clearer information for users arriving from organic search.

Internal Linking

Strengthened internal links between relevant pages to improve navigation, distribute authority and help search engines understand the relationship between key sections of the website.

Performance & Conversion Analysis

Website traffic and conversion performance were monitored to understand whether SEO activity was contributing to meaningful user engagement and business outcomes.

Key Outcome

The project combined technical SEO, on-page optimisation, keyword targeting and content improvements with ongoing traffic and conversion analysis.

This approach allowed SEO performance to be assessed alongside user behaviour rather than relying only on rankings or traffic as measures of success.
